School mode turns itelx into a student's desk. Instead of the terminal,
itelx starts School mode: today's classes and what is due, homework with
due days, priority and progress, the weekly timetable, exams with
countdowns and topics, a diary page for every school day, grades, and a
study timer for every subject (its own length, Pomodoro or a stopwatch)
that counts against the subject's weekly goal, with statistics. Tools:
calculator, unit converter, multiplication tables, typing practice, quiz.
