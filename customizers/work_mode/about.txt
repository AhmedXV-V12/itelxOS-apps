Work mode turns itelx into a workplace. Instead of the terminal, itelx
starts Work mode: a dashboard of the day, tasks on a list or a board
(backlog, to do, doing, review, done) with priorities, due days, estimates,
checklists and tasks that wait for others, projects with deadlines, budgets
and hourly rates, a timer that keeps running across restarts and a weekly
timesheet, meetings with agendas and minutes whose action items become
tasks, people and a deals pipeline, notes, invoices made from billable
time, expenses, and reports for a week, a month or a year.
