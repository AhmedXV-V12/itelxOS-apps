itelxOS apps - the USB kit

Copy everything in this folder (update.xvr and the folder dex) to the top of
a USB stick formatted as FAT32. Plug the stick into itelxOS 1.3 and answer
"yes" to "are you need update ?": the apps are installed into /dex. Then
type: welcome

Put your own .xvc and .xvr programs into dex/ too.

The guide, in English and Arabic: https://github.com/AhmedXV-V12/itelxOS-apps
